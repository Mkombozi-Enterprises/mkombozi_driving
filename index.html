Our Courses

A course for every kind of driver

NTSA groups Kenyan licences into classes based on the vehicle you'll drive. Tell us where you're headed, and we'll get you there — legally, safely, and confidently.

Class A

Motorcycles & Boda Bodas

Balance, control, and defensive road-craft for two- and three-wheelers, from mopeds to courier bikes.

Class B

Personal & Private Cars

Manual or automatic saloon car, SUV, and pick-up training — our most popular course and the right start for most learners.

Class C

Trucks & Commercial Vehicles

Light to heavy goods vehicle training for drivers building a career in transport and logistics.

Class D

PSV — Matatu & Bus

Commercial passenger-vehicle training plus PSV badge preparation for aspiring public transport drivers.

Add-On

Defensive Driving

Hazard perception and advanced safety technique for licensed drivers who want real confidence in traffic.

Add-On

Refresher Lessons

Back-behind-the-wheel coaching for licence holders who haven't driven in a while.

Prep

NTSA Theory & Test Prep

Highway Code coaching and mock tests, so you walk into your NTSA test ready.

Fast-Track

Intensive Crash Course

A condensed, fast-track programme for learners working against a deadline.
